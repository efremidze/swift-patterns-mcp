<objective>
Replace per-query YouTube `search.list` fan-out with a periodic channel catalog crawl plus local search, eliminating the tool's ~33-queries-per-day quota ceiling and ~900ms of per-query network latency.

Purpose: `searchPatternsInternal` currently spends ~300 quota units per deep query (2-3 `search.list` calls at 100 units each). The "global-first" strategy pays full price to rank across all of YouTube, then discards every result not on one of 2-3 known channels. The target corpus is a few hundred videos from a handful of creators — it belongs in a local index searched by the existing scorer.
Output: A `youtube-catalog` module that crawls uploads playlists cheaply and serves queries from disk, quota accounting, and a `patreon.ts` search path with zero query-time YouTube calls.
</objective>
